1. Introduction

Ruby Chemicals ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of individuals who use the Ruby Chemicals mobile application ("App") and associated online services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, used, stored, and protected when you use the App.

The App provides users with access to Ruby Chemicals' digital services and business-related functionalities through a secure online portal. By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the App and associated portal may process the following categories of information:

Account and Authentication Information

  • User IDs and account identifiers.
  • Login credentials and authentication-related information.
  • Information required to verify and manage user access.

Business and Operational Information

  • Information entered, submitted, viewed, or managed through the portal.
  • Records and documents associated with the services and functionalities made available to your account.
  • Other information necessary to provide the portal's functionality.

Device and Technical Information

  • Device-related identifiers and technical information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens used to deliver push notifications.
  • Basic technical information required for application functionality, troubleshooting, and security.

We only process information that is relevant to providing, maintaining, and securing the App and associated services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the App may be used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and provide secure access.
  • To provide access to available portal features and services.
  • To display and manage business-related information.
  • To deliver notifications and important service-related updates.
  • To maintain application functionality and compatibility.
  • To identify, investigate, and resolve technical issues.
  • To protect the App and associated services against unauthorized access, misuse, and security threats.

We do not use personal information collected through the App to sell products or services to users through unsolicited advertising.

4. Data Storage and Processing

The App primarily functions as a mobile interface to the Ruby Chemicals online portal.

The App does not independently maintain a separate permanent database of users' business records on the mobile device. Information submitted through or accessed within the portal may be transmitted to and processed by the associated online systems and backend infrastructure.

Some technical information, such as application cache and temporary files, may be stored locally to support normal application functionality.

Information maintained by the online portal is handled in accordance with the applicable operational, security, and retention requirements of the associated services.

5. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a service provided by Google, to deliver push notifications.

For this purpose:

  • An FCM registration token may be generated for your device.
  • The token may be transmitted to our backend and associated with your user account.
  • The token is used to identify the device to which notifications should be delivered.
  • Notifications may contain service-related announcements, updates, or other information relevant to the App.

You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

Firebase services are subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Information may be processed or shared only where necessary for the operation of the App and associated services, including:

  • Service providers: Technology and infrastructure providers that support application hosting, backend operations, and notification delivery.
  • Firebase / Google: To facilitate push notification functionality and related technical services.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: Where disclosure is required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • Security and protection: Where necessary to investigate security incidents, prevent misuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and our services.

Service providers are expected to process information only for the purposes for which their services are engaged and in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the App and associated services against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include secure communication protocols, access controls, authentication mechanisms, and appropriate infrastructure safeguards.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the associated services, fulfil operational requirements, comply with applicable legal obligations, and maintain security.

Users who wish to request deletion of their account or associated personal information may contact us using the contact details provided below.

Requests will be reviewed and processed in accordance with applicable legal and operational requirements. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business and professional use and is not designed specifically for children.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If we become aware that information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address the matter.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

Such services may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

The App may also provide access to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party websites or services.
